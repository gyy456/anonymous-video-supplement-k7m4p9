Remaining limitations

Thin chair legs can show slight ghosting, and some fine detail remains soft. Limited or occluded reference information may contribute.

Input panoramas may differ in exposure or illumination. When a long route is generated in segments, these differences can produce a brief brightness change at the joins, as illustrated below.

Scene coverage & comparison protocol

Collection 01 contains three distinct scenes; Collections 02–05 each contain one: seven scenes in total. Collections 01–04 compare six methods; Collection 05 compares eight methods. All comparisons use matched target-camera paths. These selected qualitative examples do not cover the full quantitative evaluation.

Native inputs, reference budgets and training conditions differ across methods. Displayed panoramas are representative observations, not each method’s complete reference set. Collection 05 additionally includes two-endpoint LRM results with substantially different input coverage. Apartment panoramas are reprojections of the original cube faces. Training-split information and intentional cuts are listed in the clip details.
