# Anonymous Video Supplement

Qualitative indoor reconstruction comparisons: 32 videos across six distinct scenes in five collections. Collections 01–04 compare six methods; Collection 05 compares eight. Collections 04 and 05 share a scene but show different trajectories. The gallery includes 12 original panorama images and an illustrative brightness-transition flowchart.

Collection 05 contains the selected 5.2-second corridor-to-bedroom excerpt, corresponding to 4.5–9.7 seconds of the original continuous route (156 frames at 30 fps). It does not traverse all five panorama centers of the full route. The reconstruction uses broader scene observations.

All comparisons use matched target cameras. Native inputs, reference budgets and training conditions differ. NoPoSplat receives two perspective crops; PanoSplatt3R receives two complete panoramas bracketing the excerpt. This is not an equal-input benchmark, and the selected qualitative examples do not cover the full quantitative evaluation. Relevant protocol details are on the page.

No analytics, external embeds, author links or third-party fonts are included.
